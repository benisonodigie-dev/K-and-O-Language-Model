# K-and-O-Language-Model
Urgent SMS Triage and Nearest-Care Referral for Maternal and Infant Health.
## 1. The problem
# What problem are you solving, and for whom?
Community health workers (CHWs) and Community Health Extension Workers (CHEWs) across rural areas in Nigeria receive dozens of SMS and WhatsApp messages every day from caregivers of young children, women with chronic conditions and even pregnant women. Many messages are routine, but a critical minority signal life-threatening emergencies — severe bleeding, convulsions, difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, or signs of severe dehydration and malaria complications. CHWs are often the only health contact for communities that may be hours from the nearest Primary Health Care centre. Current phone inboxes have no prioritization: urgent and non-urgent messages sit together. This project builds a lightweight text classifier that flags only the urgent messages so CHWs can respond first to women and infants whose cases need urgent care.
# Why does it matter in your context?
Nigeria accounts for the highest absolute number of maternal deaths globally and a very high share of under-five mortality in Africa. Rural women and children in northern and southern states face long distances to facilities, limited transport, and overloaded Primary Health Care centres. When a CHW can recognise and escalate an obstetric emergency or childhood convulsion within the first critical hour, survival rates rise sharply. Yet CHW phone inboxes are overwhelmed. A single delayed reply to an urgent message can put a mother’s or child’s life at risk. By automatically surfacing only messages that require immediate attention, the system protects the most vulnerable without asking CHWs to become clinicians or to follow medical advice generated by AI. The model’s sole job is to flag urgency and indicate location of the patient for quick attention — nothing more.
# What is out of scope?
Diagnosis, treatment advice, or replying to patients with medical guidance. Ambulance dispatch. Voice calls and non-SMS channels. The tool only flags urgency and suggests the nearest facility; the CHW decides what to do. We will not give advice; we will only flag urgency.
